# Mergington High School Activities

A super simple website application that allows students to view and sign up for extracurricular activities.

## Features

- View all available extracurricular activities
- Sign up for activities
- Share activities on Facebook, X, or WhatsApp

## Sharing an activity

Choose Facebook, X, or WhatsApp under an activity to open that service in a new
tab and share a link. Friends who open the link will be taken to the highlighted
activity. You do not need to log in to the school website to share; the selected
service may ask you to log in. Shared messages do not include student details.
